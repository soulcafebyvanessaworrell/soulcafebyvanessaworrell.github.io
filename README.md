# The Soul Cafe by Vanessa Worrell

The website of The Soul Cafe, an online psychotherapy practice led by Vanessa Worrell, M.A. Healing brewed daily: warm, non-judgmental therapy for adolescents, adults, couples, and families, with sessions held online.

## On the site

- **About**: who Vanessa is, how she works, and the approaches she draws on.
- **Packages**: single sessions and six-session packages for one-on-one, couples, and family therapy, priced in INR, USD, and GBP.
- **Book**: book a session, or a free 15-minute consultation to see whether it is a fit.
- **Blog**, with **Learning**, **Supper Club**, and **Merch** on the way.
- **Contact**: a message form and WhatsApp. A privacy page explains how the site handles your information.

Every page footer carries the Tele-MANAS helpline for anyone in crisis.

## Languages

The site reads in 29 languages: English, Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Urdu, Kannada, Odia, Malayalam, Punjabi, Assamese, Maithili, Santali, Kashmiri, Nepali, Sindhi, Dogri, Konkani, Russian, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Polish, Ukrainian, Romanian, and Dutch. The language menu at the top switches between them and remembers your choice. Until you choose, the site opens in your browser's language, if that page exists in it.

The English text is the practice's own. The other languages are careful machine translations awaiting review by native speakers; if a sentence reads wrong in your language, please open a translation correction issue.
