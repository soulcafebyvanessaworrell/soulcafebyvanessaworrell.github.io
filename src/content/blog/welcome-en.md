The Soul Cafe started with a simple idea. Talking to someone should feel less like walking into a clinic and more like sitting down with a warm cup and a person who has the time for you.

I'm Vanessa. I'm a psychotherapist, and I made this space for people who want to feel better but aren't sure where to begin. Some arrive carrying something heavy. Some just want a place to think out loud. Both are welcome here.

Therapy in this room gives you space to understand what you feel, at a pace that is yours. You come as you are, and we start from there. When slow is what you need, we go slowly.

This blog will hold small, honest notes: things I've learned from the people I sit with, ideas worth staying with for a while, and the odd reminder that healing is ordinary work you do a little at a time.

If any of this sounds like something you need, book a session or just say hello. The kettle is on.
